Privacy Policy

IELTS Simulator by HomiLabs · Effective 31 October 2026

IELTS Simulator keeps everything on your device. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet access of its own. We receive no data about you.

Who we are

IELTS Simulator is published by HomiLabs. Contact: homilabs.smc@gmail.com.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your test attempts: the answers you typed or chose, your Writing text, your self-check ratings, timings and the estimated bands.
  • Your Speaking recordings, if you allow the microphone: the audio of your answers in a mock test, kept so you can play them back and self-check.
  • Your settings, such as whether you chose the Academic or General Training module.

This information is saved in the app's private storage on your phone or tablet. Other apps cannot read it.

The microphone

The app asks for microphone permission only so you can record your Speaking answers. Recordings are made and played back on your device. They are never uploaded, shared or analysed by us or by anyone else. You can refuse the permission and still use every other part of the app.

What we do not do

  • The app does not request internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.
  • No account, sign-in, name, email address, phone number or location is collected.
  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services are used.
  • We do not sell or share personal data, because we do not have any.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Because nothing leaves the device, there is nothing for us to share or delete on our side.

Deleting your data

Everything is stored only on your device. To remove it, uninstall the app, or open your phone's Settings, then Apps, then IELTS Simulator, then Storage, and choose Clear storage. We hold no copy, so there is no account or server data to delete.

Backups by your device

Android may include app data in your own device backup (for example your Google backup). That is controlled by your device settings and your Google account, not by HomiLabs.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: homilabs.smc@gmail.com.
