Privacy Policy

English Language Test Simulator by HomiLabs · Effective 31 October 2026

English Language Test Simulator keeps your test attempts and recordings on your device. It has no accounts and we receive no data about you. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID; you can manage that choice in your Android settings.

Who we are

English Language Test Simulator is published by HomiLabs. Contact: homilabs.smc@gmail.com.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your test attempts: the answers you typed or chose, your Writing text, your self-check ratings, timings and the estimated bands.
  • Your Speaking recordings, if you allow the microphone: the audio of your answers in a mock test, kept so you can play them back and self-check.
  • Your settings, such as whether you chose the Academic or General Training module.

This information is saved in the app's private storage on your phone or tablet. Other apps cannot read it.

The microphone

The app asks for microphone permission only so you can record your Speaking answers. Recordings are made and played back on your device. They are never uploaded, shared or analysed by us or by anyone else. You can refuse the permission and still use every other part of the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on the home, practice and report screens, and occasionally a full-screen ad after you finish a test report. No ads are shown during a test.

  • To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, app and device information, and ad interactions.
  • Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app asks for your consent before ads load, and you can be asked again by clearing the app's storage. On Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Settings, then Privacy (or Google), then Ads.
  • Your test answers, Writing text, ratings and Speaking recordings are never sent to Google or anyone else.
  • How Google handles this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in, name, email address, phone number or precise location is collected by us.
  • We run no analytics or crash-reporting service of our own.
  • We do not sell personal data, and we receive none: ad data goes to Google, not to HomiLabs.
  • Your answers, writing and recordings stay on your device and are not used for advertising.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Everything is stored only on your device. To remove it, uninstall the app, or open your phone's Settings, then Apps, then English Language Test Simulator, then Storage, and choose Clear storage. We hold no copy of your attempts or recordings. Data held by Google for advertising can be managed in your Google account's ad settings.

Backups by your device

Android may include app data in your own device backup (for example your Google backup). That is controlled by your device settings and your Google account, not by HomiLabs.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
