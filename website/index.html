Practise like it is test day.

A test that runs like the real one

Each mock test has all four sections. Nothing is paused, rewound or extended.

Listening

Four parts, 40 questions. You hear each recording once, with time to read the questions beforehand and two minutes at the end to check your answers.

Reading

Three passages and 40 questions in 60 minutes, with the passage beside the questions on a tablet and a question navigator you can flag in.

Writing and Speaking

Writing Task 1 and Task 2 in 60 minutes. Speaking in three parts with a one-minute preparation time and your answers recorded for you to play back.

How a mock test works

  1. Choose a moduleAcademic or General Training. Listening and Speaking are the same for both.
  2. Sit the testListening, Reading, Writing, then Speaking, each on its real clock.
  3. Get your reportEstimated bands for Listening and Reading, every answer explained.
  4. Check Writing and SpeakingRate yourself against plain-English criteria and compare with Band 8 model answers.

Practice for every angle

A library of short guides and drills you can use before, between and after mock tests.

  • Question-type guides for Listening and Reading: gap fills, multiple choice, True/False/Not Given, matching and more.
  • Writing guides for Task 1 (charts, maps, processes, letters) and Task 2 essays, with Band 8 model answers.
  • Speaking guides for all three parts, with example answers and a cue-card routine.
  • Topic vocabulary lists for the themes that come up most.
  • Progress across your attempts, so you can see which section needs work.
  • Phone and tablet layouts, with the text beside the questions on larger screens.

Private by design

  • No account and no sign-in. Open the app and start.
  • Works offline. All tests and lessons are inside the app.
  • Your Speaking recordings stay on your device and are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app removes them.
  • No ads and no tracking.
